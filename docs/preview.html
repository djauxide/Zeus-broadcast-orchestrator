04Honest limitations

  • All 12 gates verified: Gate 8 (PathHealth) verified with real measured RTT; Gate 12 (drain resilience) verified on multi-node kind with live pod deletion and leader election move.
  • Fabric is bound, not flowing: MxlReceiver points at the correct pod, but without a real MXL producer there is no MxlFlowMirror and the receiver stays Pending in mxl-k8s terms.
  • No real SRT/RIST: transport code is scaffolding; the webhook rejects malformed SRT but nothing encrypts media yet.
  • Single-node dev topology: topology spread is overridden for kind, NMOS registry is not deployed, and RDMA providers are out of scope.
